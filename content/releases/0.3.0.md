### Added

- **Sensor block.** A new block shaped as a concave wedge, a curved trough with a feed held on an arm at its focus, in the dock's shield and cloak column (key **7**'s tray). It is what gives a ship **Overview mode** (below). A sensor works only with no generator within 5 blocks of it and nothing in the two cells in front of its dish; a blocked sensor still draws its 25 kW. The dock warns when no sensor on the ship is clear, and marks the sensors and what blocks them.
- **Overview mode.** Press **O** with a clear sensor for a tactical view from above, out to the sensor's 20 km. It draws 900 kW through the sensor while it is on, more than one generator gives: without the power it is refused, and a ship that loses the generators it needs drops out of it. The world keeps running, but the ship's thrust, turning and weapons are held (only **Z**, flight assist, still switches, so the ship can stop or coast, and a click never fires), the cursor is free, the dust streaks are gone and the world's sound is muffled; radio calls stay clear. Your ship is a small 3D dart that turns as it does. Every contact in range of 5 blocks or more has a bracket in its color (red hostile, green neutral, gray derelict) sized by how big it is, and a line down to your plane; ships and stations are named with their distance, rocks are faint circles, and range rings, labelled at the left, mark the plane. A list of the ships and stations in range, nearest first with their size, distance and speed, runs down the right side to the status bar: its **HOSTILE**, **NEUTRAL** and **DERELICT** switches hide those contacts from the map and the list (derelicts start hidden; your locked target always shows), the legend is the tooltip of the info icon in its header, and the arrow beside it folds the list away. The wheel zooms (in to 2 km), holding **C** turns the view (and it stays turned), a click locks the nearest bracket or a row of the list, and Tab, T and Y work as before. A cloaked ship never shows. Losing your last clear sensor (or the generator that blocks one coming back), docking or losing your pilot turns it off.

### Changed

- The arena is five times wider: 40 km across the middle instead of 8 km. The stations, the raiders' outpost and the raiders' camps stay within 20 km of the centre, a few minutes' flight apart, and the asteroid belt and its derelicts run on out to the edge.
- Saved worlds from before this version no longer load (the world file is version 17).
- The asteroid belt beyond the edge of the 40 km arena still leads into the planet's ring from anywhere in the arena.
- Distant asteroids are drawn with fewer facets, inside the arena and beyond it, so the bigger arena costs the frame rate no more than the old one did.
- Your ship now has a detection range: 10 km, or 20 km with a clear sensor. Tab, T, Y, R, the automatic lock on a ship that turns hostile, the red edge markers and Overview only find what your ship detects, and a lock ends when its target leaves detection.
- Ships, buoys, debris and derelicts beyond your detection range fade out of view, and a cloaked ship is never detected. Friendly stations are always detected, at any range; the raiders' outpost is not.
- The asteroid belt holds 240 rocks along its length, twice as many as before, and the belt beyond the edge of the arena is just as dense, so nothing changes where one meets the other.
- The asteroid belt now curves gently along the planet's ring through the whole arena, so it flows into the ring beyond the edge in one sweep.

### Fixed

- A piece of debris that breaks up again no longer names its pieces "debris debris".
- NPC salvagers no longer chase a fast-moving wreck or piece of debris off the edge of the map: they pass over anything drifting faster than 10 m/s.
