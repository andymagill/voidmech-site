Salvage, a free aim camera and better targeting.

### Added

- **Free aim camera.** The camera now follows your aim (the blue diamond) and the ship turns to wherever you look, as far round as you like. Tap **C** to switch to the old chase camera, which follows the ship, and back; hold C for free look as before. A flight starts in free aim, and the mode you choose lasts until you quit. In free aim the precision zoom looks along your aim.
- A toast above the HUD's status bar names the camera mode when you switch it.

- **Salvage.** A new **salvage beam** block (group **5**, key **5**, magenta) cuts blocks off whatever it meets, a ship's own hull included, and puts each one whole in your hold, where it counts toward its mass and capacity. A ship with a raised shield stops the beam completely; a full hold refuses the cut. The CARGO slot of the status bar shows the blocks you hold. **Y** locks the next derelict (wreck, debris, disabled ship), nearest first; Tab and T still skip them. The dock's block bar and the Controls list have the new block and key.

- **Blocks in the inventory.** The inventory panel has **Resources** and **Blocks** tabs (the panel keeps its height when you switch, and its figure columns are wide enough for six-digit masses, so nothing shifts). The Blocks tab lists every block type your hold, your storage or the market holds, with its icon and name, and an arrow pair on each row moves them between the ship and the station; blocks cross whole, one at a time, each once its mass has flowed at the dock's rate. **Unload All** now moves the ore and the blocks, and **Change Ship** puts the old ship's blocks in your storage.
- The inventory panel has a **close button** at its top right, its Resources page is the panel's full width, and Stone, Metal and Ice each have their own icon.
- **I in flight** opens the same panel as a **Cargo** view of the hold (no arrows, no Unload); an open panel frees the cursor so its tabs can be clicked. Docking or undocking with it open turns it into the full panel or back.

- **Derelict sites.** Five derelicts lie in the asteroid belt, always at least two Hulks (cut about in places) and two Capital Nacelles, with no pilot and no power. A raised shield never stops your salvage beam on them, since they have none. Cut one down with the beam; when it is mostly gone another appears elsewhere in the belt and what is left of the old one drifts as debris. **Y** takes them; Tab and T still skip them.

- A salvage beam stopped by a raised shield now flares the shield and lights its dome where it lands, so you can see it is firing.
- The block your salvage beam is cutting lights up and fades toward an unlit magenta as the cut goes on, and a block cut into the hold has its own sound.

- **NPC salvagers.** Eight **Salvagers** (the developer's blueprint: a salvage beam and two storage blocks) now work the belt. Each flies to the nearest derelict that still has a block its hold can take (a derelict site, a piece of debris, a wreck or a disabled ship, never a fragment of a few blocks or anything with a raised shield), holds off it and cuts it, and at about 60 % of its hold flies to a station, docks and unloads the blocks into the station's stock, so the **Market** column's Blocks tab fills without you. Salvagers may share a derelict, and one never fires through another ship or a station in its way. In a new world half of them start docked at the stations and half out in the belt. They are unarmed and never turn hostile.

### Changed

- **Targeting.** Tab, T and Y now lock the target closest to the one you have locked (or to your ship, with nothing locked), and each tap in a run goes one farther out, so you can walk from one target to its neighbours without bouncing back. **Hold** the key for the target closest to your ship. Y skips small scraps (under four blocks); R still locks one. The lock box stays on the edge of the screen when the target is off to the side, above or below, or behind you, instead of vanishing.
- Saved worlds from before this version no longer load (the world file is version 15).
- Under the hood, the game's ties to its engine (input, sound, files, start and quit, the flight HUD and the build dock's mouse and keys) now go through a small engine-independent layer, a first step toward other platforms. Nothing plays differently.
- An arena may now fly up to 96 NPC ships (`arena.json`; it was 48). The shipped arena flies 47.

### Fixed

- A salvage beam whose hold has no room for the block it is on no longer wears that block down; it leaves it untouched.
- Closing the World Catalog with a click while the game is paused no longer unpauses the flight.
